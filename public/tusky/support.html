How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. Enter the email you use for Tusky and send the reset email. Open the link on that phone, or, if the email shows a code instead, enter it on the same screen. Tusky then asks you to choose a new password. If the link has expired, request another one from the sign-in screen.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Paid plans are billed by the App Store or Google Play, and they renew until you cancel them there. Deleting your Tusky account does not stop the charge.

  • App Store: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tusky, then cancel.
  • Google Play: open the Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Tusky, then cancel.

Refunds are handled by the store that charged you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account & privacy → Delete my account, then confirm. This disconnects every bank and deletes the account. Cancel a subscription in the store first, if you have one. If you cannot sign in, use the account deletion page.
