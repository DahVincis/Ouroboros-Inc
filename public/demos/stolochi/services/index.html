Common Questions

Do you offer a bridal trial?

Yes — I highly recommend a trial session 1–3 months before your wedding. It's the best way to test the look, timeline, and products before the big day.

Do you travel to venues?

Absolutely. I travel to your hotel, venue, or home anywhere in the Triangle area. Travel fees may apply for locations outside the Durham/Raleigh/Chapel Hill area.

How far in advance should I book?

Bridal dates book quickly — especially for peak season (May–October). I recommend booking at least 6–12 months in advance to secure your date.

Can you accommodate the whole bridal party?

Yes! I offer group rates for bridal parties of three or more. I can also bring a second artist for larger parties when needed.
